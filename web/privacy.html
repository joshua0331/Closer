Privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Who operates Couple Court

Joshua Christian Mirang operates Couple Court. Contact joshuachristianmirang@gmail.com for privacy or deletion requests. This policy describes the court-only web experience.

Information we process

We process your chosen name, uploaded selfie, private room identifier and membership, room chat, case topic, testimony, questions, requested compensation, photo evidence, generated artwork, rulings, appeals and decisions. The current room flow does not request an email or password or create a Firebase Authentication account. We store hashed room session and invitation tokens, usage records and hashed connection identifiers to limit abuse. Hosting providers may process operational request information.

Sharing with your partner

Court records, evidence and optional courtroom artwork are shared with the other participant in your room. These records are not end-to-end encrypted: the backend processes them and authorized operators and service providers may access them as needed. A partner can take screenshots or save copies, which Couple Court cannot retrieve.

AI processing

When you confirm an AI action, relevant names, submitted testimony, requested compensation and original evidence photos are sent through our backend to Meta. Muse Spark handles questions and first rulings; Muse Wonder handles appeals. Optional SAM 3.1 processing sends the selected image and object prompt. Optional Muse Image processing sends consented portraits and the courtroom reference to generate artwork. Uploading a selfie shares it with the room participant; a separate confirmation precedes Meta processing. Room chat is not automatically sent to the AI judge; submit relevant material as testimony or evidence. Do not submit someone else’s portrait without permission. We do not use these features for identity verification or personality analysis. Provider processing and retention are governed by the provider’s applicable terms; we do not promise zero retention.

Hosting and local storage

Google Firebase and Google Cloud provide database, storage and hosting for rooms. Couple Court’s primary court database and media storage are in Singapore; authentication, delivery, logs and Meta processing may occur elsewhere. Your browser stores a private room session and invitation details to restore your seat and may cache application assets. Anyone with access to that browser session may be able to enter the room. The server stores hashes of private room credentials, not their original values. Evidence and artwork fetched by the web app are held in browser memory during use. Returning to the lobby or clearing browser data does not delete cloud records or copies saved by your partner.

Retention and deletion

Cloud records are retained until removed through an operator-assisted request or maintenance; automatic case expiry is not implemented. Expiring a demo session does not delete cases. Email us to request access, correction or deletion and include a non-secret room identifier if available. We may need to verify your relationship to the room through an appropriate secure process. Do not email private invitation links or session tokens. Access expiry is not automatic data deletion. Records from the earlier account-based app are separate and have not automatically been removed. Provider backups or operational logs may persist under their retention practices. We will explain material limits when handling your request.

Choices and scope

A chosen name and selfie are required to create or join a room. Evidence uploads, portrait generation and AI submissions are optional. You can decline a proposed ruling. The court-only interface does not request location or offer a general couple feed or memories. Chat is scoped to the private court room. Records from the earlier broader app have not automatically been deleted. You may have privacy rights depending on applicable law; contact us to exercise them. This beta is intended for adults 18 and older.

Updates

We will date changes on this page. Questions about this notice or our handling of your data can be sent to the contact above.
